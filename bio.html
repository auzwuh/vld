See my course on edX ↗
As a learner

I'd rather understand how something works underneath than memorize the answer, which is why I picked a course on the math behind robot arms instead of something I already knew.

Course I took through VLD

Robotics Foundations I: Robot Modeling, a self-paced edX course from the Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II.

I haven't switched courses so far.

What I hoped to gain

A real look at robotics before university, and practice running my own learning without someone checking on me.

My quote
"It always seems impossible until it's done." Nelson Mandela
My advice for a Grade 10 student starting this course

Write your journal entry the same day. If you wait a week, it's hard to say anything specific, and the entries are worth the most when they are.
